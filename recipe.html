Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, peppers, tomatoes and spices.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Peppers
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil
  • Onions
  • Spices

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onions.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil and cook the blended mixture.
  4. Add the rice, water, salt and spices.
  5. Cover and cook until the rice is soft and properly cooked.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
